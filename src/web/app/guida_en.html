1. What OfficinaONE is

OfficinaONE is the Windows and Linux version of the Fiat Ducato eLearn electronic workshop manual, which originally ran only from CD with Internet Explorer. It contains all the data from the CD (11 languages, over 3,400 entries, more than 5,000 images and wiring diagrams): after installation the CD is no longer needed and no Internet connection is required.

2. Start: home page, model and language

OfficinaONE always starts from the Model selection page, which is the home page (the same one opened by «Home page»). Below the picture you find the recently opened pages, your favourites and the program news:

  • the Fiat, Peugeot and Citroën logos remind you that the manual applies to the Fiat Ducato 244 series and its twins Peugeot Boxer and Citroën Jumper: click any of them to enter the manual;
  • on the left, in the language menu, you can choose among 11 languages (Italian, English, German, Spanish, French, Dutch, Portuguese, Czech, Polish, Greek, Turkish);
  • alternatively, click the picture or the name DUCATO.

4. Engine and production period

The two menus below «DUCATO» determine which documents are shown:

  • Engine: 2.0 16V, 2.0 JTD 8V, 2.3 JTD 16V, 2.8 JTD 8V;
  • Production period: «up to 02/05» or «from 03/05».

When you change one of these choices the page is reloaded with the contents valid for that vehicle. Entries that do not concern the chosen engine do not appear in the lists.

If the message saying that the selected engine and production validities do not match valid documents appears, that page does not exist for the chosen engine/period (for example a component found only on JTD engines): change the engine on the left or use the link to start browsing again.

5. Browsing the manual

  1. Choose a section from the menu on the left.
  2. The list of entries, sorted by code, appears in the central area: click the code or title to go down one level.
  3. When you reach a final entry you see its content (text, tables, images).

To go back use the ◀ Back button, the ◀ arrow at the top left, or click a level in the navigation line. Blue links inside the text open the linked page; in that case a «Back to …» link also appears at the top.

7. Variants «Applies to versions with»

Some parts of the content apply only to certain versions (e.g. right-hand drive, air conditioning, Panorama). In the lists the variant is shown in brackets after the title; in the contents the relevant part is enclosed in a black frame, preceded by the words «Applies to versions with: …».

8. Procedures: Removal and Refitting

In the procedures each Removal step has a link to the corresponding Refitting step and vice versa. Clicking the link scrolls the page to the linked step and highlights it briefly in yellow. If the linked step exists only for another engine, a notice appears with a button to switch to that engine. Tables with the spanner icon list the special tools with their codes.

9. Electrical system and wiring diagrams

When you open an electrical function (e.g. E3010 Horns) the tabs Description, Functional description, Wiring diagram, Component location and Print electrical function (prints all tabs together) appear at the top.

Moving around the diagram

  • the mouse wheel scrolls the page even over the diagram; Ctrl + wheel zooms the diagram in/out at the pointed position;
  • click on an empty point of the diagram: opens it enlarged in a separate window, where the wheel zooms;
  • drag with the left button to move the diagram; double-click to return to the full view;
  • buttons below the diagram: arrows to move, +/− to zoom, = for the initial view;
  • Enlarge image: opens the diagram in a large window with the list of components on the right; clicking a component code opens its page.

Below the diagram the Components table links each code (e.g. B001, D001) to the related connectors/earths/junctions page.

10. Images

When you move over a photo or drawing the pointer becomes a magnifying glass: click to open the image in a separate window where you can:

  • zoom with the wheel or with +/−, move by dragging, Fit to the window, 1:1 actual size, ⟳ rotate;
  • Print: prints only the image with the title of the procedure;
  • PDF: saves only the image to a PDF file.

11. Searching the manual

Type a word in the «Search word or code…» box on the left and press Enter, or click Search in the top menu. The results appear in the central area:

  • grouped by section, in the order of the manual tree;
  • for each result the full category path is shown (each level can be clicked);
  • the searched word is highlighted; the in the text tag means that the word is in the page content and not in the title, with an extract of the text.

You can search several words together (all must be present), search a code (e.g. E3010, C001, 1004B), limit the search to some sections or exclude the text search. Capitals and accents do not matter. The search takes the selected engine and production period into account and tolerates typing errors and common synonyms (Italian manual).

12. Favourites

In the left column: ☆ Add to favourites saves the open page (it becomes ★ Remove from favourites); ♥ Favourites shows the list, from which you can open or delete (✕) the saved pages. Favourites are kept after closing the program.

13. Print and Save as PDF

  • Print (or Ctrl+P): prints only the content of the central area, without menus.
  • Save as PDF: creates an A4 PDF of the displayed page, with title and page numbers, and opens it at the end. The file name is suggested from the code and title of the entry.
  • For electrical functions use the Print electrical function tab: it produces a document with all tabs (description, diagram, components…) ready for printing or PDF (landscape orientation recommended).

14. Text size and resuming

The page adapts automatically to the screen size. To enlarge or reduce: Ctrl++ / Ctrl+−, Ctrl+0 to return to the standard size. The choice is remembered. When reopened, OfficinaONE remembers language, engine, period and window size.

16. Frequently asked questions

I cannot find an entry I am sure exists.

Check engine and production period on the left: many entries apply only to some engines. Also try the search with «also search the page text» enabled.

Clicking a component in the diagram shows a «validities do not match» message.

That component is not documented for the selected engine. Change it on the left or go back with «Back».

Do I need the CD or the Internet?

No: all data are included in the installation. Only spare parts, video guides and updates use the Internet.

How do I close the program?

With Exit in the top menu, with the red ✕ in the top left bar or with the window's X.

17. Spare parts and video guides online

The orange 🛒 Spare parts online item, at the bottom of the left menu, opens the list of parts that can be bought online, divided by category. Each card shows name, code, description, price, a preview photo and the Buy online ↗ button, which opens the shop in the browser. The box at the top filters the list as you type; the star ☆ adds an item to your favourites.

Just below, the red ▶ Video guides item collects tutorial videos by category: each card shows the preview, the title and a short explanation. A click opens the video in a separate, resizable window.

These are the only sections updated via the Internet: each time you open them OfficinaONE downloads the latest list. Without a connection the last saved copy is shown, with its date. When new items are available the menu shows a «N new» label.

18. Tabs, notes, history and tree

Tabs

You can keep several pages open at the same time, as in a browser. Tabs appear in the top bar:

  • Ctrl+click (or middle-click) on a link or a tree item: opens the page in a new tab;
  • ＋ or Ctrl+T: duplicates the current page in a new tab;
  • ✕ on the tab, middle-click or Ctrl+W: closes the tab; Ctrl+Tab: goes to the next tab.

Personal notes

Above each content there is the link 📝 Add a personal note to this page: write your notes (checked torques, parts used, remarks) and press Save note. The note appears in yellow at the top of the page, is included in prints and PDFs and is saved on the PC.

History

🕘 History in the left column lists the last 30 pages viewed.

Navigation tree

Below the section selected in the left menu the tree of entries appears, opened up to the current page (highlighted in orange). Click ▸ to open a branch and the title to open the page; a second click on an open category or on the selected section closes it.

19. Workshop and night modes

In the top bar on the right:

  • A+ — workshop mode: much larger text and buttons, easy to read from a distance on a monitor or tablet at the bench;
  • ☾ — night mode: content area with a dark background, less tiring in poorly lit places (photos keep their real colours).

The choices are remembered; click the button again to turn them off.

20. Updates and reports

At start-up (and every 6 hours) OfficinaONE checks whether a new version is available. If so, a window with the news appears: Update downloads and installs the update automatically and reopens the program; Later shows it again at the next start-up. You can check at any time with Check for updates in the bottom bar. Updates download only the program: the manual data stay on the PC.

If something does not work use ✉ Send a report in the bottom bar: an e-mail opens already filled in with version, open page and technical information; add the description of the problem and send it.

21. Anonymous statistics

To know how many people use OfficinaONE and which versions, the program sends once a day (and at each installation or update) an anonymous message with: a random PC code, the program version, the type of event, the Windows version and the language. No name, e-mail, files, pages viewed or other personal data are sent.

22. Workshop tools

Above each procedure there is a bar with:

  • ☑ Checklist — turns the numbered steps into boxes to tick: click a step when you have done it. The state is saved; ↺ reset starts again.
  • 🖨 Job sheet — opens in a new tab a printable version with vehicle data, tools, steps to tick, space for parts and signatures. Print it or save it as PDF from the top menu.
  • ▶ Presentation — shows one photo and its steps at a time, full screen; arrows or space bar to advance, Esc to exit.
  • ◫ Side by side — splits the window in two to consult two pages together (e.g. procedure and wiring diagram); drag the orange bar to change the proportions.
  • ⚠ Report error — prepares an e-mail with the exact reference of the page.

Below the bar, when present, you see the special tools mentioned in the procedure (code and description) and the linked spare parts and videos: to link them, add a Codice operazione (operation code) column in the Spare parts and Video Excel files and write the operation codes (e.g. 1048B10, or 1048 for the whole group; several codes separated by commas).

In the left menu: ⚡ Quick sheets collects the most consulted data (maintenance plan, capacities, products, tightening torques, tyres, engine data, timing…); 🧰 Tools contains unit converters, a glossary of abbreviations, the wire colour legend and backup/restore of your data.

In the top bar the ⏱ timer measures working time: click to start/stop, double-click to reset. In the manual pages technical abbreviations (ABS, EGR…) are underlined with dots: hover over them for an explanation. In wiring diagrams the 🎨 Wire colours button shows the colour legend.

23. Logbook, maintenance and photos

📒 Logbook and maintenance (left menu) is the logbook of your vehicles: create one or more vehicles with registration, chassis number, year, km and engine. ✔ Use this vehicle in the manual immediately sets the right engine and period.

  • Maintenance due dates: the intervals are taken from the manual's scheduled maintenance plan. When you carry out a job click ✔ done: the next due date is calculated from km and date. Items due or overdue are coloured and a reminder appears on the home page.
  • Work carried out: record date, km, description, parts, hours and cost. Clicking in the Hours box suggests the timer value. At the bottom there is the total of costs; 🖨 Print logbook prints it.

Personal photos: below the bar of each procedure, 📷 Add your own photos attaches photos you took (before/after, details of your vehicle). Click to enlarge them, ✕ to delete them. They are included in the backup.

PDF of a chapter: in list pages (e.g. «18 CLUTCH») the 📄 PDF of the whole chapter button creates a single PDF with cover, contents and all pages of the chapter for the chosen engine.

After each installation or update OfficinaONE automatically checks the integrity of the manual data.

24. Advanced wiring diagrams

  • Following a wire: click a conductor in the diagram and its whole path, up to the connected components, turns orange. Click an empty point to remove the highlighting.
  • Finding a component: above the component table, below the diagram, there is the 🔎 Search component box: type a code (e.g. B001) or a name to filter the list.
  • Searching the legends: on the Search page the photo legends and components only option finds the pages where the word or code appears in the numbered photo legends or in the component tables.

25. Interface language

When you choose a language on the home page, besides the manual also the OfficinaONE menus, buttons and windows are translated into that language. This guide, the news and the glossary are available in Italian and English (used for all other languages).

26. Portable version

Besides the installer, OfficinaONE-Portable (.zip file) is available: extract it to a USB stick or any folder and start OfficinaONE.exe, without installing anything. Notes, favourites, logbooks and settings are saved in the OfficinaONE-dati folder next to the program, so you can take them from one PC to another. In the portable version «Update» opens the download page of the new version.

27. Ducato 1986 (first series)

Next to the Ducato 244 on the home page you will find the Ducato 1986 range (first series, "290"): the original Fiat technical service manual (Italian), with colour scanned pages. The package (about 105 MB) is included in the full installer; after an update it is downloaded once on first use and then works offline. Use the engine selector on the left to show only the chapters for your engine, Previous/Next or the ← → keys to turn pages, and Search to find words in the text of every page (recognised automatically from the scans). Favourites, history, notes and photos work as in the 244. At the end of the sections you also find the German workshop manual (Reparaturhandbuch, 338 pages, third-party publication) with an Italian translation of every page below the scan, including the maintenance plan and 40 wiring diagrams.

28. New Ducato X250

On the home page you also find the New Ducato X250 (2.2, 2.3 and 3.0 JTD): the original eLearn manual with all the features of the 244, plus the Diagnosi Examiner section. The package (about 200 MB) is included in the full installer; after an update it is downloaded once on first use.
